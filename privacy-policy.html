Privacy Policy

How consultation-request information is collected and shared through this website.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who operates this site

This website is operated for Sridhar Swamy Ch, offering Vedic astrology and numerology consultations online and in person in Jagtial and Hyderabad, India. For privacy enquiries, complaints or requests relating to information you share for consultations, contact WhatsApp +91 94419 31386 or call +91 94419 31386.

Information you choose to provide

The booking form asks for a service, full name, consultation mode, preferred date and language preference. You may optionally include a question and birth information such as date, time and place of birth. You may also share information directly in a WhatsApp conversation. Do not submit another person’s birth details without suitable permission.

How your information is used

Information supplied in a booking request is used to understand your enquiry, communicate about a consultation and, where applicable, provide the requested astrology or numerology service. Booking details entered in the form are prepared as a WhatsApp message; the form does not itself confirm an appointment.

WhatsApp and external services

When you press “Send to WhatsApp,” your browser opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. Your information is sent only when you choose to send it there. WhatsApp/Meta processes messages and related information under its own terms and privacy policy. The website also loads externally hosted fonts, icons and CSS resources, which may receive technical information such as your IP address. The website links to Instagram. Please review the relevant providers’ policies.

Cookies, logs and analytics

This website does not include its own advertising pixels or analytics scripts in the provided HTML. It uses third-party content delivery services for fonts, icons and styling. Your hosting provider and these services may receive technical data, such as IP address, browser details, requests and access logs, and may use cookies in accordance with their own policies. If additional analytics or advertising services are introduced, this policy will be updated and applicable consent choices provided.

Information sharing

The consultation request is prepared for WhatsApp and is only sent once you send the message in WhatsApp. Your information may be viewed by the consultation provider for the purpose of addressing your enquiry. WhatsApp and other technical providers process information according to their separate privacy terms. We do not sell your birth information or consultation enquiries.

Storage, retention and protection

The website booking form does not itself save the text you enter in a website database. If you send the prepared message, your enquiry and any later consultation messages may remain in WhatsApp conversation history and related device or service backups. Information is kept as needed to manage consultation requests, provide agreed services, address disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information under our control; requests will be reviewed and handled subject to applicable retention requirements. You may also manage messages on your own WhatsApp account.

Your choices and requests

You may choose not to provide optional birth details. To ask about access, correction, withdrawal of consent or deletion of information held by the consultation provider, contact the published WhatsApp number. Deletion requests may be subject to legal and operational requirements; information already handled by WhatsApp is governed by its own processes.

Children and third-party birth information

Parents or guardians should handle enquiries involving children. Please do not submit a minor’s information or another person’s birth details unless you have the authority and any consent required under applicable law.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Contact: Sridhar Swamy Ch, Jagtial & Hyderabad, India; phone/WhatsApp +91 94419 31386.
