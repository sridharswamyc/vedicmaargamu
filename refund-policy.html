Refund & Cancellation Policy

Information to review before confirming or paying for a consultation.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Current booking process

The current website sends booking enquiries through WhatsApp and states that consultation fees are available on request. The website does not itself collect payments or automatically confirm appointment slots.

Confirm the terms before paying

No payments are processed through this website. Before you make a payment for a consultation arranged via WhatsApp or phone, ask for written confirmation of the fee, included service, scheduled time, delivery method, cancellation and rescheduling conditions, and any refund terms. Please do not make payment unless you agree to those conditions.

Cancellation and rescheduling requests

If you need to cancel or reschedule, contact +91 94419 31386 on WhatsApp as early as possible, quoting the booking details. A request alone does not guarantee the change until confirmed.

Refund requests and applicable terms

For any advance payment made after a booking is agreed, the cancellation and refund conditions confirmed in writing before payment will apply, subject to your rights under applicable law. No blanket “no refund” rule is imposed by this website. If the consultation provider cannot deliver an agreed paid service, contact the provider to request an alternative appointment or an appropriate refund. Payment concerns and refund requests can be made on WhatsApp with the consultation details and payment reference. Any approved refund will be arranged through a suitable available payment method, with the timing communicated when it is approved.

Complaints and statutory rights

For service problems, cancellations or payment disputes, call +91 94419 31386 or contact the provider on WhatsApp. This policy does not limit any applicable consumer-protection rights.
