Important information on predictions, remedies, compatibility readings and professional advice.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Traditional, interpretive guidance

Vedic astrology, Kundali analysis, Dosha Pariharas, Muhurtham, gemstone suggestions and numerology are traditional and interpretive practices. Their claims and predictive accuracy are not established by scientific evidence. Different practitioners may reach different interpretations.

No guaranteed outcomes

No prediction, gemstone, remedy, name change, business-number suggestion or consultation is guaranteed to produce a particular personal, relationship, health, career or financial outcome. Statements about auspicious timing and compatibility are opinions within traditional systems, not assurances of future events.

Not medical, legal or financial advice

Content and consultations do not diagnose illness, treat medical or psychological conditions, provide legal representation or constitute regulated financial or investment advice. Seek qualified professional support for decisions in these areas, and do not delay professional treatment because of an astrological reading.

Marriage and relationship compatibility

Astrological matching should not be the sole basis for marriage or relationship decisions. Consider personal choice, consent, communication, compatibility and other relevant circumstances.

Personal responsibility and applicable rights

Consultation information is provided to help users reflect on questions and traditions. Users make their own decisions. This disclaimer does not take away statutory consumer rights or excuse inaccurate or misleading service representations.
